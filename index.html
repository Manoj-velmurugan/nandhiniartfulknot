Our Story

Artistry in
Every Stitch

"Embroidery is painting with thread" — at Nandhini Artfulknot, we live this truth every day. Our skilled artisans pour passion and precision into each design, whether it's a resplendent bridal blouse or an intricately beaded saree border.

We believe in preserving the rich heritage of Indian embroidery while crafting pieces that feel utterly contemporary and personal. Each order is treated as a unique canvas.
